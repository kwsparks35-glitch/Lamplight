# Lamplight support

For general help, first consult `README.md`, `DEPLOYMENT.md`, and open GitHub
issues. Reproducible nonsecurity defects may be filed with the Bug Report issue
form after removing personal information.

Privacy or private support questions may be emailed to
**Canon.Series.Packets@gmail.com**. Do not send study-backup files, passwords,
health information, signing material, or other sensitive data.

Security vulnerabilities must follow `SECURITY.md` and should never be posted
publicly before coordinated remediation.

Lamplight does not provide emergency, pastoral, medical, mental-health, or
legal services. If someone may be in immediate danger, contact local emergency
services or an appropriate trusted person rather than relying on the app.
