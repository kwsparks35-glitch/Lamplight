# World English Bible notice

Lamplight identifies its embedded Scripture corpus as the World English Bible
(WEB). The WEB project states that its Scripture text is in the public domain
and may be copied, published, distributed, quoted, translated, adapted, and
used without payment of royalties.

“World English Bible” is a trademark of eBible.org. Lamplight uses that name
only to identify the translation and does not claim sponsorship or endorsement
by eBible.org.

Authoritative project information:

- <https://worldenglish.bible/>
- <https://worldenglish.bible/faq.htm>

The inherited app did not preserve the exact upstream source archive and
revision used to create the embedded corpus. That provenance must be recorded
before release; see `docs/CONTENT_PROVENANCE.md`.
