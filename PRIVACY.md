# Lamplight Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** August 14, 2026

**Policy version:** 1.2

Lamplight is designed to work without an account and to keep study data under
the learner's control. The application does not include advertising,
analytics, telemetry, behavioral profiling, or third-party tracking SDKs.

## Data stored by Lamplight

Lamplight stores the information needed to provide its study features,
including study sets, review history and due dates, settings, streaks, game
progress, and earned oil. This information is stored locally in the browser or
on the device. Lamplight does not intentionally transmit this study data to
Kenneth W. Sparks, Canon Series, advertisers, or analytics providers.

No account, name, birth date, precise location, contact list, camera,
microphone, advertising identifier, or payment information is required.

## Network activity

The installed iOS, Android, and Linux builds are intended to include the
Scripture text and application assets. Lamplight does not intentionally make
third-party content, advertising, or analytics requests.

The web/PWA edition must contact the website from which it is hosted to load
the application and check for updated app files. Like most web hosting, that
host may process ordinary request information such as IP address, user agent,
requested file, and time of access for delivery, security, and operational
logs. Lamplight does not put study sets or review progress into those requests.
The host's own privacy and retention terms also apply.

Apple, Google, Linux distributors, operating-system vendors, and app stores
may independently process download, purchase, update, crash, or diagnostic
information under their own settings and privacy policies. That processing is
not controlled by Lamplight and must not be confused with data collected by
the app itself.

## Backups, export, and deletion

Learners can create a manual backup file from inside Lamplight. The learner
chooses where that file is saved and whether it is copied to email, cloud
storage, or another service. Those destinations have their own privacy terms.

Device, browser-profile, or operating-system backups may also copy local app
data if the learner has enabled such backups. Removing the app or clearing its
site data usually removes the active local copy, but it may not remove copies
from device backups or manually exported files.

The in-app **Reset all local data** control deletes Lamplight's active study
data on that installation after confirmation. A learner should also delete
any exported backup files and platform backups they no longer want.

## Support messages

If a learner voluntarily sends a support email, the message contains whatever
the sender chooses to include. Contact information and message content are
used only to respond, investigate the request, maintain appropriate security
records, and meet legal obligations. Do not send Scripture backup files,
passwords, health information, or other sensitive personal information in a
support request.

The email provider processes messages under its own terms. Support messages are
retained only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, investigate recurring
problems, protect the project, and meet legal obligations. A sender may request
deletion at the contact below, subject to those limited needs.

## Children

Lamplight is a general-audience educational application and does not knowingly
collect personal information from children through the app. A parent or
guardian should supervise a child's use of external email, cloud backup, app
store, and web-hosting services.

## Security and changes

Lamplight minimizes privacy risk by avoiding accounts and intentional study-
data transmission. No software, device, or storage system can be guaranteed
perfectly secure. Material policy changes will update the effective date and
policy version in this document and in the published app.
