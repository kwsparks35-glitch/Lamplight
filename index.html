Lamplight

Scripture memory — whole passages, kept whole. Free, offline, and quiet.

Lamplight is a free Scripture-memory app: gather passages into study sets, review them on a gentle spaced schedule, and practice with recall games. No ads, no accounts, no tracking — your study stays on your device.
