Support

Help for a free, offline Scripture-memory companion.

Lamplight is a free, offline-first app for memorizing whole passages of Scripture. It has no accounts, no ads, and no tracking. If something isn't working or you have a question, here is how to reach a person.

Email support

Canon.Series.Packets@gmail.com

Please do not send study-backup files, passwords, health information, or other sensitive data in a support message.

Common questions

Do I need an account? No. Lamplight works fully offline and stores your progress only on your device.

Will it cost anything? No. Lamplight is free permanently — no purchase price, no in-app purchases, no subscriptions, and no advertising.

How do I keep my progress if I change devices? Use the in-app backup export to create a file you control, then import it on the new device. Your data is never uploaded for you.

How do I clear my data? Use Reset all local data inside the app. Remember to also delete any backup files you exported.

Reporting a problem

When you email, it helps to include your device model, Android version, the app version, and a short description of what you expected and what happened. Reproducible, non-sensitive bug details let us fix issues faster.

A note

Lamplight does not provide emergency, pastoral, medical, mental-health, or legal services. If someone may be in immediate danger, please contact local emergency services or an appropriate trusted person rather than relying on the app.
