Privacy Policy

Your study stays with you.

Effective date: August 14, 2026  ·  Policy version: 1.2  ·  This page presents the policy from the Lamplight repository's PRIVACY.md and covers every edition of Lamplight (Android, installable web app, and any future desktop or iOS builds); platform-specific notes are marked where they apply.

Lamplight is designed to work without an account and to keep study data under the learner's control. The application does not include advertising, analytics, telemetry, behavioral profiling, or third-party tracking of any kind.

The Android app of Lamplight ships every Scripture and asset inside the app and requests no internet permission at all. It cannot send your study data anywhere, because it makes no network connections.

Data stored by Lamplight

Lamplight stores the information needed to provide its study features, including study sets, review history and due dates, settings, streaks, game progress, and earned oil. This information is stored locally on your device. Lamplight does not transmit this study data to Kenneth W. Sparks, The Canon Series, advertisers, or analytics providers.

No account, name, birth date, precise location, contact list, camera, microphone, advertising identifier, or payment information is required or requested.

Network activity

The installed Android app includes the Scripture text and application assets and does not make third-party content, advertising, or analytics requests. The optional installable web version of Lamplight must contact the website that hosts it to load the application and check for updated app files; like most web hosting, that host may process ordinary request information such as IP address, user agent, requested file, and time of access for delivery, security, and operational logs. Lamplight does not place study sets or review progress into those requests, and the host's own privacy terms also apply.

Google, Android, and the Google Play Store may independently process download, update, crash, or diagnostic information under their own settings and privacy policies. That processing is not controlled by Lamplight and is separate from data collected by the app itself, which is none.

Backups, export, and deletion

You can create a manual backup file from inside Lamplight. You choose where that file is saved and whether it is copied to email, cloud storage, or another service; those destinations have their own privacy terms. Device or operating-system backups may also copy local app data if you have enabled such backups. Removing the app or clearing its data usually removes the active local copy, but may not remove copies from device backups or manually exported files.

The in-app Reset all local data control deletes Lamplight's active study data on that installation after confirmation. You should also delete any exported backup files and platform backups you no longer want.

Support messages

If you voluntarily send a support email, the message contains whatever you choose to include. Contact information and message content are used only to respond, investigate the request, maintain appropriate security records, and meet legal obligations. Please do not send Scripture backup files, passwords, health information, or other sensitive personal information in a support request. The email provider processes messages under its own terms, and support messages are retained only as long as reasonably necessary to respond, investigate recurring problems, protect the project, and meet legal obligations. You may request deletion at the contact below, subject to those limited needs.

Children

Lamplight is a general-audience educational application and does not knowingly collect personal information from children through the app. A parent or guardian should supervise a child's use of external email, cloud backup, app store, and web-hosting services.

Security and changes

Lamplight minimizes privacy risk by avoiding accounts and intentional study-data transmission. No software, device, or storage system can be guaranteed perfectly secure. Material policy changes will update the effective date and policy version in this document and in the published app.
